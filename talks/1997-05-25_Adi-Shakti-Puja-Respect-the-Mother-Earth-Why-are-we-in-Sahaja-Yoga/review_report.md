### Critic Filter

| Source | # | Verdict | Reason |
|--------|---|---------|--------|
| L | L1 | **Keep** | «Тепер» is an adverb, not a parenthetical; what follows is the pronoun «те», not a subordinate clause or an inserted word, so nothing licenses the comma. Internal consistency (¶34 has no comma) and corpus precedent — earlier reviews removed the same English-calque comma. |
| L | L2 | **Keep** | Codified rule: a comma separates two adjacent conjunctions when no «то/тоді» follows. The translator applies it correctly elsewhere in this very text («що якщо поїдуть туди, то…» ¶25 and «бо якщо … тоді» ¶38 — correctly without a comma because the correlative is present). Same fix kept in earlier reviews («бо, якщо ви реалізовані душі…»). |
| L | L3 | **Keep** | Same rule as L2: the subordinate clause can be lifted out without restructuring («…, і ви позбуваєтеся їх»), and no «то» follows. Mid-sentence, so the exemption for a sentence-initial linking conjunction («Тож якщо…», «Але якщо…» — left untouched in ¶33, 41) does not apply. |
| L | L4 | **Keep** | Genuine punctuation error: homogeneous predicates with one subject joined by a single «і» take no comma. Kept in several earlier reviews. Every other «…, і» in the text closes a subordinate clause or a participial phrase, or links clauses with different subjects — those are correct and untouched. |
| L | L5 | **Keep** | Here «зрештою» renders “after all” and is parenthetical; ¶50 already commas it, while ¶11 («і зрештою пробилася крізь Кайлаш» = “ultimately”) is adverbial and correctly has none. The fix restores both the rule and internal consistency; same ruling in earlier reviews. |
| L | L6 | **Keep** | Genuine government error, not taste: «потрібно» + infinitive governs the dative, and «від вас потрібно поширювати» is a contamination of «від вас потрібне (щось)» and «вам потрібно (робити)». It also invites the wrong reading “spread from you”. The fix is minimal, keeps the «потреба / потрібно» thread of the passage, and matches the EN. |
| L | L7 | **Remove** | Defensible as written: «Кундаліні, Аді Кундаліні» is a restated, refined subject (a homogeneous series), exactly parallel to «бажання, цілковите бажання» in the same sentence, and the source has the same shape. Not a mandatory comma. |
| L | L8 | **Remove** | Euphonic alternation is a recommendation. «Ввібрати» is a codified dictionary variant of «увібрати» and is used consistently (4×, ¶33–34); «з» before a sibilant was ruled trivial in an earlier review. No error. |
| L | L9 | **Remove** | Lexical preference, outside the orthography/grammar/punctuation mandate. The phrase is understandable spoken register and faithful to “just made out of nothing but soil”. |
| L | L10 | **Remove** | False positive: with «скільки» and «більшість» + genitive plural, a plural predicate is permitted when the individuals act separately — which is the sense in both sentences. |
| S | S1 | **Keep** | Deity-pronoun consistency is an explicit criterion. The translator capitalizes Mother Earth’s pronouns everywhere else, deliberately overriding the source’s “it”, and earlier reviews confirmed uppercase for Mother Earth. The single lowercase «на ній» is an oversight. |
| S | S2 | **Keep** | The source’s casing merely wavers (“Realization … realization … Realization … realization” in consecutive, parallel sentences about the same act). The glossary permits the capital form, the rest of this transcript is uniformly uppercase, and earlier reviews kept the same consistency fix. |
| S | S3 | **Remove** | Not pronouns standing for the Deity: «самій» is an attributive intensifier rendering EN lowercase “itself”, and «сама по собі» is a set adverbial phrase describing the ordinary seeker’s Kundalini (“though in itself is pure”). Contrast ¶16 «Мати Земля Сама піднялася», which renders EN “Herself” and is uppercase. |
| S | S4 | **Remove** | Not an error: Правопис allows the capital for the highest church office (Папа Римський). Single occurrence, so no internal inconsistency. |
| S | S5 | **Remove** | Precedent (2004-05-09 Sahasrara Puja and 2004-07-04 Guru Puja reviews): no canonical form exists, the glossary is silent, the corpus is split four ways. A per-talk change would be arbitrary; needs a corpus-wide decision. |

## Summary

- Language (L): 10 issues found, 6 approved by Critic
- SY Domain (S): 5 issues found, 2 approved by Critic
- Total corrections applied: 8 (9 edits — S2 covers two occurrences in ¶53)

The translation is of high quality and faithful to the spoken source,
including Shri Mataji’s self-corrections and broken-off sentences. Deity
pronouns, sacred terms and glossary terminology are consistent throughout,
and all punctuation characters follow the Ukrainian orthography rules. The
applied fixes are five punctuation corrections (one English-calque comma
after «Тепер», two commas at a junction of conjunctions, one superfluous
comma between homogeneous predicates, one parenthetical «зрештою»), one
verb-government fix («вам потрібно»), and two capitalization-consistency
fixes (Mother Earth pronoun «на Ній»; «Реалізації» ×2). All discretionary
style items — euphony, lexical preference, optional agreement and comma
variants — were filtered out by the Critic.

Not changed, for a human decision: the place-name spelling
«Кабелла-Лігуре» (S5), which is split four ways across the corpus.
