## Process

Reviewed `transcript_uk.txt` (full paragraphed Ukrainian text) against `transcript_en.txt`
using 2 reviewers (L – Language, S – SY Domain) + 1 Critic filter, per
`templates/language_review_template.md`.

- "Paragraph" = line number in `transcript_uk.txt` (lines 1–4 are the header).
- Mechanical checks run on the whole file before the read-through: no Latin/Cyrillic
  mixing, no ASCII apostrophes, no `""`/`„"` quotes, no `—` or ` - ` dashes, no `…`
  character, no double spaces, no space before punctuation, all `«»`, `()` and `[]`
  balanced. All clean.
- Spellings of names and mantras were checked against the rest of the corpus
  (`talks/*/transcript_uk.txt`) where the glossary is silent.

### Critic Filter
| Source | # | Verdict | Reason |
|--------|---|---------|--------|
| L | L1 | Remove | Reads as a spoken restart («And the festivals, all the festivals of the world must be celebrated»), not a set-off clarification; no closing comma is required. |
| L | L2 | Keep | Codified rule: a comma separates two adjacent conjunctions when the subordinate clause can be dropped and no «то» follows («бо ви народжені як брати й сестри» stands on its own). |
| L | L3 | Keep | Same rule; «навіть якщо…» is a removable concessive clause, comma goes before «навіть». |
| L | L4 | Remove | і/й after a vowel is a euphony preference, not an error; «і» is acceptable here. |
| L | L5 | Keep | Same rule as L2; «Тому що» is a subordinating conjunction, so the sentence-initial exception for і/а/але does not apply. |
| L | L6 | Keep | «Насамперед» is an adverb, not a parenthetical; the same text leaves it without a comma in par. 27, 42 and 45, and the corpus does so 15 times out of 17. |
| L | L7 | Keep | A colon cannot separate a subordinate clause from its main clause. A dash is permitted there for intonational emphasis and keeps the pause of the original «is:». |
| L | L8 | Keep | «З собою» at sentence start produces the з+с cluster the orthography steers away from; the text uses «із собою» twice already, so this is also an internal inconsistency. |
| L | L9 | Remove | Intonational dash in an elliptical sentence is acceptable; not an error. |
| L | L10 | Keep | Same rule as L2 («тому що ваше зростання не відбудеться» stands on its own). |
| L | L11 | Remove | «і те й інше» is not explicitly listed among the comma-free set phrases; both spellings are in normative use. No clear justification. |
| L | L12 | Keep | Same rule as L2. |
| L | L13 | Remove | After the coordinating «але» the comma before a subordinate conjunction is not required; the original is correct. |
| L | L14 | Keep | Same rule as L2; without the comma the double «що про що б» is also hard to parse. |
| S | S1 | Keep | The pronoun's referent is Shri Krishna; the capitalization rule for individual Incarnations is explicit and does not depend on who is speaking. |
| S | S2 | Remove | The rule covers Shri Mataji and Incarnations; the English has no pronoun here, and lowercase is a legitimate reading. |
| S | S3 | Remove | English is «the truth part of it» – the true part of a problem, not absolute Truth. Lowercase is correct. |
| S | S4 | Keep | The «ґ» convention is for Sanskrit; «ґ» for a foreign [g] is allowed in personal names (Ґевін) but not in toponyms, and every other English place name in the corpus uses «г». |
| S | S5 | Remove | «Я Деві Сарва Бхутешу» is the established corpus spelling (8 occurrences, no «Йа»). Changing it here would create an inconsistency. |
