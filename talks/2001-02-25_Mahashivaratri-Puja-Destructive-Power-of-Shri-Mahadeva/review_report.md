| L | 5 | Remove | Not a clear error. «з ним … він» can refer to «хтось» (masc.), the speaker named earlier in the same sentence. It also keeps the spoken "he". |
| L | 6 | Remove | A style preference. «правдиво» is understood correctly here and does not change the meaning. |
| L | 7 | Keep | A real punctuation error. It is the only place in this text, and in the whole corpus, that puts a comma before a one-word «чого/чому/як». |
| L | 8 | Keep | A real punctuation error. There is no comma between predicates joined by a single «і». |
