### Critic Filter

| L | 1 | Keep | Ukrainian punctuation rule: no comma between homogeneous subordinate clauses joined by a single «і». Both clauses depend on the one «коли». |
| L | 2 | Keep | A real grammar error: examples after «як-от» must agree in case with the word they explain. The fix keeps the meaning and wording of the original. |
| L | 3 | Keep | A real grammar error: «решта» is a singular collective noun, so «усі решта» is wrong. «Усі інші» keeps the meaning of «All the rest». |
| L | 4 | Keep | Comma rule where two conjunctions meet: no «то» follows, and the «якщо» clause can be removed without rebuilding the sentence. The comma is required. |
| L | 5 | Remove | The sentence breaks off («…крізь...»). We cannot tell whether a «то» would have followed, so the comma rule cannot be applied with confidence. |
| L | 6 | Remove | «Хоч (хоча)… але» is an accepted concessive pair in Ukrainian. It also mirrors the spoken «Though…, but still». |
| L | 7 | Remove | A style preference. «З точки зору» is widely accepted in standard Ukrainian. |
| L | 8 | Remove | It reflects the spoken «very huge crowd» (Fidelity to the Spoken Original). It is not a grammar error. |
| L | 9 | Remove | The quoted command is addressed to an individual. EN «Go inside» does not show number. Not an error. |
| S | 1 | Remove | Shri Mataji said «Sahaj Yog» here, and the glossary allows «Сахадж» (`Sahaj / Sahaja` → «сахаджа / сахадж / Сахаджа / Сахадж»). Keeping it is faithful to the spoken original. |
| S | 2 | Remove | False positive. The «I» is the seeker looking in the mirror («Mother is like a mirror… better get rid of it and go ahead»), not Shri Mataji speaking of Herself, so lowercase is correct. |
