## Process

- No double spaces, no space before punctuation, and no missing space after punctuation
- Only `«»` quotation marks are used (no `„"`, `""` or `“”`), and `«`/`»` are balanced on every line
- Every dash is a spaced en-dash ` – `. There are no em-dashes and no hyphens used as dashes.
- Ellipses are written as `...` with no space before them, with no Unicode `…` and no double or quadruple periods
- Apostrophes are all U+2019 (`’`)
- Parentheses and square brackets are balanced
- `ґ` is used throughout in SY terms (Ґуру, Ґіта, Ґанеша, йоґ-), and the forms «Сахаджа Йозі», «сахаджа йоґи/йоґів/йоґами» are correct

- Deity pronouns are capitalized: Shri Krishna (Він/Свого/Своїх/Ти/Тобі), Radha (Вона, «(Мене)»), and God in the donkey story (Він)
- Spiritual terms are capitalized: Дух/Духа, Істина/Істини, Інкарнацією, Пуджа Ґуру / Деві Пуджа, Дхарма санньясі, лотосових Стоп

### Critic Filter
| Source | # | Verdict | Reason |
|--------|---|---------|--------|
| L | L1 | Keep | Ukrainian punctuation rule: homogeneous members joined by a **repeated** conjunction (і…і, чи…чи, або…або) are separated by commas before each repetition («А, чи Б, чи В»). The same text already follows this rule correctly in P13 («на красиві речі, або на красивих жінок, або…»). |
| L | L2 | Keep | When a subordinating conjunction is immediately followed by another one («бо коли»), a comma goes between them unless the main clause continues with «то/тоді/так». No «то» follows here. |
| L | L3 | Keep | Same rule as L2 («що, коли …, цей океан»). The translator applies it correctly where «то/тоді» does follow (P20 «що коли …, то»; P68 «що коли …, тоді»; P53 «що якщо …, тоді»), so these lines are inconsistent as well as wrong. |
| L | L4 | Remove | «або навіть» introduces an additive, intensifying member, and Ukrainian punctuation allows a comma before such constructions («і навіть», «а також», «або навіть»). The comma is justified and this is not an error. |
| L | L5 | Remove | Style preference only. «до кого б не…» is accepted in modern Ukrainian. The text varies the two forms («Хоч би ким ви були», «Що б ви не робили»), but neither form is wrong. |
| L | L6 | Keep | This is a genuine grammar error. Pronouns with «-небудь» are not used under negation, which requires the negative «ні з ким». «ні з ким іншим» keeps the meaning of «with Me or anyone» and adds nothing to the content. |
| L | L7 | Keep | Same rule as L2: «що якщо» with no «то» in the main clause. |
| L | L8 | Remove | Not certain enough to be an error. «не уявляти про щось» in the sense «не мати уявлення» is widespread and acceptable in the spoken register this transcript reproduces. The Critic does not approve doubtful changes. |
| L | L9 | Keep | Same rule as L2: «що коли ви Ґуру, ви також мати» has no «то/тоді». |
| L | L10 | Remove | Faithful to the spoken original «a very unique discovery». Per `glossary/AGENTS.md` («Fidelity to the Spoken Original»), roughness in what was said is not repaired. |
| L | L11 | Remove | Grammatical: the infinitive «бачити» is the subject («бачити … дає Мені велику радість»). The change would be a style preference. |
| S | S1 | Remove | The EN transcript itself capitalizes «Saints» at exactly this point, for emphasis, and the translation mirrors it. This is not a glossary-rule violation, only a stylistic choice that follows the source. |
| S | S2 | Remove | Mirrors the EN, which writes «the Divine power» and then «this divine power». Lowercase «божественний» as a plain adjective is correct, as with «божественні люди» (P22). |
| S | S3 | Remove | False positive. `terms_lookup.yaml` prescribes lowercase «бандхан», and the translation follows the glossary. |
| S | S4 | Remove | False positive. The EN says exactly «a person who doesn't have a weak mind or a weak heart». Under the Fidelity rule a claim stays as spoken, even where another meaning seems intended. |

## Summary

- Language (L): 11 issues found, 6 approved by Critic
- SY Domain (S): 4 issues found, 0 approved by Critic
- Total corrections applied: 6

Overall, the translation is of high quality. It uses SY terminology consistently and in line with the glossary, capitalizes deity and Shri Mataji pronouns correctly throughout, and keeps typography clean (`«»`, ` – `, `...`, `’`). The translator's bracketed additions («[Це]», «[яких]», «[інший]», «[кимось]», «[людина]», «[олія]», «[приклад]», «[сфера]», «[її]») follow the Fidelity rule. Spoken roughness is preserved and not repaired. The only real errors were missing commas between adjacent conjunctions (4 cases), a missing comma with a repeated conjunction (1 case), and one negative-concord error.
