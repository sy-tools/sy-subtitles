# Language Review – 1996-07-16_Shri-Mahalakshmi-Puja-We-have-to-live-like-one-family, 2026-11-23

## Process

2+1 agent review (Reviewer L + Reviewer S + Critic) of `transcript_uk.txt`
against `transcript_en.txt`, `glossary/AGENTS.md`, `glossary/terms_lookup.yaml`,
and `glossary/terms_context.yaml`, per `templates/language_review_template.md`.

First pass — no prior review exists for this talk.

Paragraph numbers are `transcript_uk.txt` line numbers (header lines 1–4,
body starts at line 6).

Mechanical pre-checks: clean — no Latin/Cyrillic mixing, no double spaces, no
space before punctuation, quotes «» only (no `„"` / `""`), dash ` – ` (U+2013)
throughout (no hyphen-as-dash, no em-dash), apostrophe `’` (U+2019) only,
ellipsis `...` (one instance, ¶21, correct). Hyphens only in correct compounds
(по-перше/по-друге/по-третє, будь-хто/будь-що/будь-який/будь-кого, як-от,
піди-но). Lowercase first-person forms (я/мені/мій) occur only for regular
people — Nachiketa (¶25–26), the old woman (¶40), the lady (¶51) — and in the
interjection «Боже мій» (¶42); all correct.

Checked and found correct (no findings): Shri Mataji's pronouns are uppercase
throughout, including the reflexives «по Собі», «для Себе» (¶36) and «Моя мати»
(¶46). Pronouns for Lakshmi (Своїх руках, Своїй руці, Вона, Її), Shri Rama,
Sita, Raja Janaka and Mahamaya («Це Вона випробовує») are uppercase. Regular
people are lowercase: Nachiketa, the old woman, the father, the lady, the
thief. Shri Rama's «твого/ти» to the old woman is correctly lowercase.
«Стіп» (Janaka's Feet, ¶22–23) is uppercase. Glossary terms match:
Лакшмі, Махалакшмі, Махамайя, Набхі чакра, Сваха, Сахасрара, Самореалізація/Реалізація,
Раджа Джанака, Рамаяна, Сіта, Лакшмана, Шрі Рама, Ґруха Лакшмі, Раджа Лакшмі,
одержимий, блокування («буде заблокована», ¶19), Сахаджа Йоґа (locative
«Сахаджа Йозі»), and the сахаджа йоґ/йоґи/йоґів/йоґам/йоґом forms. «Свадха»
follows the dh→дх convention. «Істина» is uppercase. Language names: only
«англійська» in the header, lowercase. Speaker label «Йоґ:» is the corpus
majority form. Translator's additions in square brackets ([це], [люди],
[усе], [себе], [цей стан], [його], [вони], [така], [оголошення]) are minimal
bridges, and the spoken roughness (“the sour ber”, “he says that, somebody
said”, the Lakshmana sentence order) is preserved.

### Critic Filter

| Source | # | Verdict | Reason |
|--------|---|---------|--------|
| L | 1 | **Keep** | A subordinate clause must be closed by a comma; a dash cannot stand in for it in a complex sentence with a conjunction. When subordinate clauses precede a main clause opened by «це», the norm is «, –». The transcript applies this in ¶17 and ¶30, so ¶16 is an inconsistent slip. |
| L | 2 | **Keep** | «що:» + a first-person quote mixes indirect and direct speech, which is a recognised grammatical error; previous reviews approved the same fix. This is not a repair of the original. The English “that” is a quotative filler, and dropping it keeps every spoken word of the quote intact. The inserted clause in dashes now ends at the colon, which also introduces the direct speech. |
| L | 3 | Remove | Defensible: a sentence-initial «І» before the shared subject can be read as the first member of a repeated «і…і» («І він співав, і танцював»). The comma marks that reading and the spoken pause. Not a clear error. |
| L | 4 | Remove | Whether a sentence is exclamatory depends on intonation. The EN transcript punctuates both quotes with a period, and an evenly spoken «Як мило з твого боку.» is not wrong. Style choice. |
| L | 5 | Remove | False positive: СУМ-11 lists «прибор» = “набір предметів… столовий прибор”. «столові прибори» is normative; «приладдя» is a preference. |
| L | 6 | Remove | The colon marks the topic shift in the spoken sentence (“For Sahaja Yogis, one has to realise”). Without it, «Для сахаджа йоґів людина має усвідомити» misreads. This is a deliberate stylistic device, not an error. |
| L | 7 | Remove | Writing «не» separately with a predicative adjective/adverb that negates a property («це не важливо») is permitted by the 2019 Правопис. Not an error. |
| L | 8 | Remove | Outside L's scope (a translation nuance, not orthography or grammar). «навіть» intensifies slightly but keeps the meaning and claim spoken. Trivial. |
| L | 9 | Remove | Colloquial but understandable, and it mirrors the spoken “What more can you give?”. Style preference. |
| S | 1 | **Keep** | The only lowercase instance against 9 uppercase ones in the same transcript. Capitalization is target-language orthography, not spoken content, so normalising it repairs nothing in the original. Makes the term consistent within the text and with «Принцип Ґуру». |
| S | 2 | Remove | Mirrors the EN “Realised soul” exactly. The form is attested in the reviewed corpus (11×) and fits this transcript's uppercase «Реалізація». The glossary pair does not settle a mixed-case source. Not a clear error. |
| S | 3 | Remove | False positive: Lakshmana is an Incarnation (of Shri Shesha) in Sahaja Yoga. The rule for individual Incarnations (uppercase singular) applies, and the text applies it consistently to Him throughout ¶42–43. |
| S | 4 | Remove | False positive: the EN itself capitalizes Raja Janaka (“He was a king”, “His feet”), and the translation uses uppercase for Him consistently. The single lowercase “him” in the EN is a transcriber variant. |
