### Critic Filter
| Source | # | Verdict | Reason |
|--------|---|---------|--------|
| L | 1 | Keep | `як полум’я` is the nominal part of the compound predicate after `виглядають`. Such comparisons are not set off by commas. |
| L | 2 | Keep | Genuine agreement error: the same clause addresses a plural `вас` / `з вами`, and the transcript uses plural predicates for this audience throughout (`ви стаєте дуже великими`, ¶30). |
| L | 3 | Keep | Two real defects in one sentence: inconsistent gender agreement for one subject, and an attachment that makes the mother the challenger, which misstates the original. The closing dash restores the structure, and `кидав` matches `хто … кидав`. The meaning is unchanged and nothing is added. |
| L | 4 | Keep | Same rule as L1 (`стаєте як небо` is the predicate). The interjection `Я казала вам` stays set off. |
| L | 5 | Keep | Punctuation norm for interrupted direct speech, and it makes the transcript consistent with ¶33. |
| L | 6 | Keep | `як потяг` is the whole predicate of `вони`. A comma between subject and predicate is an error. |
| L | 7 | Keep | Clear Правопис rule for church names. The repo corpus is mixed but mostly lowercase. |
| L | 8 | Remove | Stylistic. Participles in `-юч-` are discouraged but widely used and understood, so this is not an error. |
| L | 9 | Remove | Stylistic, same as L8. It is the last item in a series of adjectives, and recasting it would disturb the rhythm of the line. |
| L | 10 | Remove | «в одному за одним» is an understandable and attested variant of the idiom. This is a style preference. |
| L | 11 | Remove | When one noun is shared by two numerals, it agrees with the nearer one (`двох людей`), which is acceptable. This is a style preference. |
| S | 1 | Remove | False positive. The capitalised reflexive for Shri Mataji is an established convention, and the transcript applies it consistently: all three occurrences with `Я` are uppercase, and there is no lowercase counterpart. |
| S | 2 | Remove | False positive. This is the Sahaja Yogi's self-questioning that continues «Що я дав Сахаджа Йозі?», not Shri Mataji speaking of Herself, so lowercase is correct. |
| S | 3 | Remove | Faithful to the source, which has "Divine power" (lowercase, ¶38) and "Divine Force… It" (capitalised, ¶39). |
| S | 4 | Remove | The glossary gives `Avatar / Avatara → Аватара` (capitalised), and `Аватар` is its correct genitive plural. |
| S | 5 | Remove | `glossary/AGENTS.md` requires an uppercase «Істина» for absolute Truth, so the original is correct. |
