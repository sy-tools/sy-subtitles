Sync status

The .sync-cloud chip — one elegant cloud, three tones. Blue progress (two sync arrows), green when synced, red on error. The edit + marker chips both use it; glyphs come from syncCloudSvg(), so this catalog can't drift from the app.

Export menu

Everything a reviewer takes away from a video, behind one icon button in the preview header: the subtitle file, and the video with those subtitles burned in. .export-pop wraps the button and the menu and is the containing block the menu is positioned against — so the menu opens directly beneath the button, floats over the page on --z-dropdown, and moves nothing. The caret is a rotated square, so the menu's own border carries on around it.

It carries no dismiss control: a click anywhere outside the anchor closes it, the button is its toggle, and Escape closes it too. Closing keeps a running render — reopening picks the polling back up.

The video item is the progress readout

The second row is not a button beside a progress panel. It wears one of four faces — create, building… please wait, download, or absent for a session that cannot dispatch — and the render's track, times, run link and error line are that row's own continuation, indented to its text.

The track is four phase segments with visible seams, each as wide as its share of the work (5 / 15 / 70 / 10). The long middle is visibly the long middle: the form itself says "three quick certainties, then one patient stretch". Fills reuse --sync-progress, the same in-progress tone as the sync cloud, and the render segment advances in ten crisp increments as ffmpeg's own progress gates complete.

The meta line is dot-separated and every dot is earned: the separators are ::before on adjacent non-empty spans, so a missing elapsed or remaining time leaves no stray dot. In this narrow a menu the phase takes its own line and the two times share the next. The remaining time is the only estimated wording — faintest AA-passing --fg5, softly worded, and not italic, because it is extrapolated from a measured rate rather than guessed.

A render runs for ~25 minutes, so the component is announced as well as drawn — and it is one control, not four: the track is the role="progressbar" carrying aria-valuenow, the segments are aria-hidden, the phase line alone is a polite live region (the elapsed counter ticks every 5s and must stay out of it), and the error line is role="alert". A failed phase is marked on the segment body, so it shows even at zero fill.
