Sync status

The .sync-cloud chip — one elegant cloud, three tones. Blue progress (two sync arrows), green when synced, red on error. The edit + marker chips both use it; glyphs come from syncCloudSvg(), so this catalog can't drift from the app.

Burn track

Progress for a burned-in subtitle render — one track split into four phase segments with visible seams, each as wide as its share of the work (5 / 15 / 70 / 10). The long middle is visibly the long middle: the form itself says "three quick certainties, then one patient stretch". Fills reuse --sync-progress, the same in-progress tone as the sync cloud, and the render segment advances in ten crisp increments as ffmpeg's own progress gates complete.

The status line is dot-separated and every dot is earned: the separators are ::before on adjacent non-empty spans, so a missing elapsed or remaining time leaves no stray dot. The remaining time is the only estimated wording — faintest AA-passing --fg5, softly worded, and not italic, because it is extrapolated from a measured rate rather than guessed.

A render runs for ~25 minutes, so the component is announced as well as drawn — and it is one control, not four: the track is the role="progressbar" carrying aria-valuenow, the segments are aria-hidden, the phase line alone is a polite live region (the elapsed counter ticks every 5s and must stay out of it), and the error line is role="alert". A failed phase is marked on the segment body, so it shows even at zero fill.
